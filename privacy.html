Privacy, in plain words

TAB has no server. There is no database, no account and no analytics.

Where your group lives

The whole group — people, bills, splits — is packed into the part of the link after #g=. It is compressed and encoded in your browser. It is never uploaded. The # fragment is not sent to any server, including the one that serves this page.

Local memory

Your browser keeps a small list of recent groups in localStorage so you can reopen them on this device. Clearing site data, or pressing “Clear” on the start page, removes it. The list never leaves your device.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, emails or passwords.
  • No payment processing and no bank connections.
  • No tracking pixels, cookies or analytics.
  • No third-party API calls. Fonts load from Google Fonts; the app itself is static files.

What to know before sharing

Anyone with the link can read the group and change it; the link is the group. It also means the group is only as private as the place you paste it. Send it to the group chat, not to a public post.
